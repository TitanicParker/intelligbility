# GPT Refractory Foundations

This folder is a plain-language derivational route into the Constraint Grammar of Completed Intelligibility.

It is **not** a reproduction of the Persistence repository and it is **not** a replacement for the canonical theory. Its purpose is narrower:

> to lead a GPT from simple statements about intelligibility, relation, completion, transmission, and persistence into the factorized grammar without asking the model to accept the mature architecture in advance.

The sequence is deliberately cumulative. Each document should be read as adding only what the previous documents have earned.

## Reading order

1. `00_refractory_ground.md`  
   Establishes the starting vocabulary: relation, constraint, completion, carrier, persistence, transmission, and minimal structural agency.

2. `01_first_derivation_transmission_requires_factorization.md`  
   Shows why transmissible intelligibility requires three distinguishable functions: change, enoughness, and continued availability.

3. `02_second_derivation_three_functions_are_factors.md`  
   Shows that the three functions are not merely three descriptions of one process. They can vary separately and therefore qualify as factors.

4. `03_third_derivation_recurrent_values.md`  
   Introduces the current recurrent values of the three factors in plain language: six Transformation Patterns, three Completion Topologies, and three Persistence Modes.

5. `04_fourth_derivation_composition_and_54.md`  
   Shows what follows when the recovered values are composed: a 6 × 3 × 3 coordinate grammar with 54 formal positions, without confusing 54 positions with 54 meanings.

6. `05_reflexive_constraint_on_evaluation.md`  
   Establishes the reflexive point: any criticism or evaluation of the grammar is itself an act of intelligibility and therefore cannot simply pretend that the phenomenon under examination is absent from the act of examining it.

7. `06_gpt_refractory_positioning.md`  
   A compact positioning document for use with a GPT. It tells the model how to preserve cumulative reasoning, how to object properly, and how not to reset the inquiry by substituting stronger claims for weaker ones.

## Governing discipline

These documents distinguish four levels:

- **plain observation** — what can be noticed without the mature grammar;
- **derivation** — what follows from those observations;
- **recovered architecture** — the current factor values developed in the wider project;
- **open claim** — what remains to be shown rather than silently assumed.

The point is not to make disagreement impossible by decree.

The point is to make disagreement **locatable**.

If a GPT disagrees with a conclusion, it should identify the first statement or inference it rejects rather than jumping downstream to a generic reservation.

That is what “refractory” means here.
